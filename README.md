# [AI Career Helper DEMO]

## 1. Problem Statement 
**Target Audience:** Graduating college students, job seekers facing difficulties in the employment market, and high school or early university students planning their future career paths.

**The Problem:** In an oversaturated job market, candidates often struggle to identify positions that realistically match their specific qualifications (e.g., GPA, certifications like ADsP, or internships). 
Furthermore, receiving constructive, data-driven feedback on interview habits (such as filler words) and optimizing portfolios for high-reaching goals remains a high-barrier task for many individuals.

**The Solution:** Our web application provides a comprehensive career management solution. By leveraging personal data as AI prompts, the system filters for "realistic" job openings and offers advanced AI analysis. 
Key features include an **AI-driven Interview Assistant** that analyzes audio files for speech habits (e.g., "um," "ah") and tone flow, and a **Portfolio Optimizer** that generates tailored cover letter drafts and suggests improvements for competitive applications.

---

## 2. Key Features (A-Project Requirements) (Temporary)
- **Advanced AI Analysis:** Real-time analysis of interview audio files to detect filler words and visualize vocal tone/flow.
- **Media Interaction:** Users can upload and manage resume files, portfolios, and audio recordings.
- **Personalized Recommendations:** Realistic job matching based on user-inputted data (Education, Experience, Certifications).
- **Secure Authentication:** Robust login system using **Google OAuth** for secure user data management.
- **Dual-User Roles:** - **Standard User:** Manage profiles, search for jobs, and receive AI feedback.
  - **Admin:** Manage site content, monitor AI performance, and update job databases.
- **Responsive Design:** Fully optimized for both desktop and mobile browsers.
